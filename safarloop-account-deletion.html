SafarLoop Account Deletion Request

SafarLoop users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your SafarLoop account, please contact SafarLoop support using the contact method provided on this page and include the mobile number or email address associated with your account.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Account and login information
  • Saved profile information
  • SafarLoop rewards associated with the account
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Booking and Transaction Information

Some information related to completed bookings, tickets, payments, refunds, or transactions may be retained for a limited period where required for accounting, legal, tax, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Such information will be deleted or anonymized when the applicable retention period expires.

Processing Your Request

We will review your request and process the deletion of eligible account data. Some information may be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate legal obligations.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Contact

For account deletion requests, please contact SafarLoop support using the official support contact details shown on your website or app.
