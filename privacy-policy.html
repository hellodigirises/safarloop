SafarLoop Privacy Policy

How SafarLoop collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our app.

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the SafarLoop mobile application and related ride-booking services (the “Service”). SafarLoop provides route search, ride bookings, driver trip management, trip notifications, and related features. In this Policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the SafarLoop service operator.

Contact: admin@safarloop.in. Before publishing, confirm that this address is monitored and replace it with the correct legal business name and contact details if needed.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: name, email address, phone number, account identifier, and account role. Driver profiles may also include vehicle information and a UPI ID.
  • Ride and booking information: route, pickup and drop-off points, travel date and time, selected seats, booking status, cancellation or modification details, passenger/driver coordination details, and trip history.
  • Payment information: payment method and status, amount, and payment-provider transaction identifiers. Online payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not ask you to enter your card or UPI credentials into SafarLoop; Razorpay handles payment credentials under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Location information: precise driver location while a driver uses location features for an active trip, subject to device permissions. Location may be shared with people who need it for that trip, such as the relevant passenger. Route and place searches may also send entered locations to Google Maps services.
  • Device and app information: push-notification token, app/device identifiers and technical information, diagnostics, crash information, and app interaction or performance events collected through Firebase/Google services and advertising SDKs.
  • Information you choose to provide: support messages, ratings, preferences, and profile image selection. The app may let you select an image from your device; selection alone does not mean SafarLoop uploads it.

2. Why we use information

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
  • Search routes and schedules; create, manage, and fulfil bookings and trips.
  • Coordinate passengers and drivers, including trip status and active-trip location features.
  • Process payments, refunds, rewards, and related records.
  • Send booking, trip, service, and security notifications.
  • Respond to support requests, maintain safety, prevent fraud or misuse, and resolve disputes.
  • Understand app reliability and usage, diagnose crashes, and improve the Service.
  • Show and measure advertisements or rewarded ads where enabled.
  • Meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

3. When and with whom information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed to provide the Service, as described here, or as required by law:

  • Drivers and passengers: booking and contact details needed to coordinate a booked trip. Active-trip location may be shown to relevant trip participants.
  • Google Firebase and Google Maps: authentication, database, messaging, analytics, crash/performance diagnostics, and place or route services used by the app.
  • Razorpay: online payment processing and payment-related transaction information.
  • Google AdMob: ad delivery, frequency management, and measurement where ads are enabled. Ad services may process device identifiers and usage information under Google’s applicable policies and settings.
  • Service providers: providers acting for us to host, secure, support, or operate the Service, subject to their role and applicable terms.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, or address fraud, security, or safety issues.

These providers may process information on servers in countries other than your own. Their handling of information is also subject to their own privacy notices.

4. Location and device permissions

Location permission is used for driver trip-location features. Location access should be granted only when the operating system asks and the feature is being used. You can change or revoke location permission in your device settings; doing so may disable live trip-location features. Camera access is used to scan passenger booking QR codes. Notification permission is used for push alerts. Photo/library access is used only when you choose a profile image. Phone access may be used to place a call you initiate, including an emergency call shortcut.

SafarLoop should not collect driver location when it is not needed for an active trip. If a release build collects location while the app is in the background, the app must clearly explain that before requesting background permission, and the Play listing and permission declarations must accurately describe it.

5. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate operational purposes, including maintaining booking and payment records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it is kept. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or take steps to remove identifying details, subject to legal and operational requirements.

To ask how long a particular category is retained, contact us using the details below.

6. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your SafarLoop account and associated personal data by emailing admin@safarloop.in from the address associated with your account. Include your account phone number or email so we can locate the account; do not send passwords or payment credentials. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting.

We will explain any information that must be retained for legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution reasons and delete or de-identify other information when the request is completed. Deleting the app from your device does not itself delete your account. If you cannot email us, contact us at the address below for another way to submit a request.

7. Security

We use access controls and service-provider security measures intended to protect information. Information sent between the app and our services is generally transmitted over encrypted connections where supported. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please keep your sign-in credentials private and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

8. Children

SafarLoop is a general ride-booking service and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate steps. Parents or guardians should arrange travel and bookings for minors.

9. Your choices and rights

You can update some profile information in the app, manage app permissions in your device settings, and control notification or advertising settings through the app/device where available. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise those rights. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

10. Third-party services and links

The Service relies on third-party services, including Firebase/Google, Google Maps, Razorpay, and AdMob. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. The app may open external websites or services; review their privacy notices before providing information to them.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the app or applicable requirements change. We will update the effective date above and, when changes are material, provide notice in the app or through another appropriate channel. Continued use after an update means the revised Policy applies going forward, subject to applicable law.
